EczemaDetect

Check a skin photo against six types of eczema

Upload a close, well-lit photo of the affected skin. A neural network trained on about 7,000 images estimates whether it looks like eczema, which type it most resembles, and shows where in the photo it was looking.

  • Fill most of the frame with the affected area
  • Use daylight, no flash, and keep the camera steady
  • One area per photo

Warmer colours in the heatmap mark the regions that pushed the model toward its answer. If the hot spot is on the background rather than the skin, don't trust the result.
